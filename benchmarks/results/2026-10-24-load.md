# Load scenarios — 2026-10-24

Go 1.27.3, darwin/arm64, Apple M5 Max, GOMAXPROCS=8, production commit
`d810da1`, benchmarks from `95112b0`. The scenarios are described in
[the README](../README.md#load-scenarios); details are in the
[environment record](2026-10-24-load-environment.json).

**Noise.** Other processes loaded the host, mainly repeatedly restarted
codebase-memory-mcp indexers. The 1-minute load average was 3.90–4.78 during the
scenarios and 3.43–3.64 during the ablation (5-minute: 4.11–4.31 and
3.99–4.03). Each scenario ran 20 rounds of `-count=1`, so the engines alternate;
the ablation ran 10 rounds. Treat differences under about 10% as unresolved.

## Eight goroutines submitting

`BenchmarkConcurrentSubmit`, ns/task, median (10th–90th percentile):

| Workload | Workers | channel | go-worker | ants | pond |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Noop | 1 | 85 (81–89) | 518 (361–670) | 229 (222–243) | 871 (856–906) |
| Noop | 8 | 46 (44–53) | 421 (403–437) | 944 (931–965) | 603 (586–625) |
| SHA2564KiB | 1 | 1,424 (1,405–1,444) | 1,753 (1,705–1,874) | 1,856 (1,839–1,893) | 1,908 (1,871–1,932) |
| SHA2564KiB | 8 | 452 (442–479) | 710 (684–798) | 1,900 (1,849–2,039) | 801 (778–856) |

With eight submitters, go-worker is the fastest of the three pools except Noop
with one worker, where it is 2.3× slower than ants and its samples spread
widely. ants slows down most with concurrent submitters: with eight workers it
takes 944 ns per Noop task here against 412 ns with a single submitter in the
[same day's engine comparison](2026-10-24-actor.md#c-engines-at-d810da1).

Raw runs: [all engines](2026-10-24-load-concurrent.txt), [load](2026-10-24-load-load.txt).

## Round-trip latency

`BenchmarkLatency`, 8 workers, Noop; each caller submits one task and waits.
Medians across the 20 rounds:

| Callers | Engine | p50 | p99 | p99.9 | ns/op | allocs/op |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | channel | 167 ns | 2.4 µs | 8.8 µs | 293 | 1 |
| 1 | go-worker | 333 ns | 3.5 µs | 9.6 µs | 577 | 3 |
| 1 | ants | 167 ns | 2.3 µs | 8.5 µs | 317 | 2 |
| 1 | pond | 1,000 ns | 4.0 µs | 11.5 µs | 1,322 | 8 |
| 32 | channel | 667 ns | 46 µs | 105 µs | 405 | 1 |
| 32 | go-worker | 1,375 ns | 262 µs | 7.4 ms | 1,454 | 3 |
| 32 | ants | 16,000 ns | 396 µs | 0.78 ms | 1,278 | 2 |
| 32 | pond | 13,416 ns | 129 µs | 0.23 ms | 701 | 7 |

ns/op is the inverse of closed-loop throughput. With 32 callers go-worker has
the lowest median of the pools but a p99.9 of about 7 ms: 18 of 20 rounds fell
between 6 and 8 ms, while ants and pond stayed within 0.2–0.8 ms every round.
That tail is consistent, not noise, and its cause is not yet known.

Raw runs: [all engines](2026-10-24-load-latency.txt).

## Ablation: acceptDone in the caller's select

Each `Schedule` call selects on the master's shared `acceptDone` channel while
handing off its task. The `noaccept` tree removes that case
([patch](2026-10-24-load-ablation-noaccept.patch)); it breaks shutdown and is
for measurement only. go-worker, 32 callers, medians of 10 rounds:

| Tree | p50 | p99 | p99.9 (range) | ns/op |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| base | 1,312 ns | 276 µs | 7.4 ms (2.0–8.4) | 1,435 |
| noaccept | 1,313 ns | 395 µs | 5.9 ms (2.0–6.7) | 1,408 |

Removing the shared channel lowers p99.9 somewhat but does not remove the tail
and does not improve p99, so it explains only part of it. Finding the rest
needs an execution trace of the slow round trips.

Raw runs: [base](2026-10-24-load-ablation-base.txt),
[noaccept](2026-10-24-load-ablation-noaccept.txt), [load](2026-10-24-load-ablation-load.txt).
