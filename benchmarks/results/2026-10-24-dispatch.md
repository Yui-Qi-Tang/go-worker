# go-worker without the central dispatcher — 2026-10-24

Go 1.27.3, darwin/arm64, Apple M5 Max, GOMAXPROCS=8. Same workloads and task
counts as the protocol in [the README](../README.md), go-worker engine only.
The four trees ran in one session in the order develop, step 1, step 2,
autoscaling, then reversed, so each case has ten samples. Medians are shown;
p-values are benchstat Mann–Whitney U tests, n=10.

| Tree | Commit | Pool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| develop | `64f0c46` | Central dispatcher and worker tokens |
| step 1 | `9ec6fd3` | Workers take tasks from the master; fixed pool |
| step 2 | `3f00498` | Adds min–max scaling; benchmark keeps a fixed pool |
| autoscaling | `3f00498` | Same code, `WithWorkers(1, n)` instead of `(n, n)` |

Every go-worker tree after develop uses 3 allocations and about 256 bytes per
task; develop used 3–4 allocations and 298–409 bytes.

## Notes

- Step 1 against develop: Noop −73% (1 worker) and −54% (8 workers),
  SHA2564KiB −33% to −37% (all p<0.001).
- Step 2 against step 1, both fixed pools: Noop 8 workers −2.4% (p=0.001),
  Noop 1 worker +7.7% (p=0.019), other cases not significant. An earlier
  session measured Noop 1 worker with no difference (p=0.645), so step 2's
  effect on fixed pools is within run-to-run variation.
- Autoscaling against step 2: Noop 8 workers +24% (p<0.001); other cases not
  significant. The cost is idle tracking, the idle timer, and growth from one
  worker inside each sample.
- Wait100us varied more than usual in this session for develop and step 1
  (confidence intervals up to 8%).
